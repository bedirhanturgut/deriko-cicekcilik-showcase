# Deriko Çiçekçilik — Management System

A real-world stock, sales and accounting management system developed for **Deriko Çiçekçilik**.

## Overview

The system was designed around the daily operational needs of a florist business.

It brings sales, stock, expenses, customer accounts and management workflows together in a single interface.

## Key Features

- Quick sales
- Product and stock management
- Stock purchasing / incoming inventory
- Expense tracking
- Accounting dashboard
- Customer accounts
- Supplier management
- Internal notes
- Admin and employee workflows
- Mobile-friendly usage
